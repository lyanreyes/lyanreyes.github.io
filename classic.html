Machine learning · Web app · 2026

ScanDex: Pokémon Card Scanner

Point your phone camera at a Pokémon card and ScanDex identifies it among 20,617 cards, shows its market price and saves it to a digital binder.

  • How: six image fingerprints with a learned logistic-regression ranker, plus an OCR check of the card number
  • Results: 71% top-1, 80% top-5, and 97% precision on matches it auto-accepts. Written up as an IMRaD paper
